Privacy, licences & disclaimer

Where your data goes

Wander is a web page that runs in your browser. It has no server, account, database, cookies or analytics of its own, and nothing you do is sent to, or kept by, the people who made or published it. To work, though, your browser contacts a few free public services directly, and they receive your IP address plus the following:

  • Map pictures — OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org): the map areas you view, and when you export a PDF.
  • Walking routes — OpenStreetMap foot router (routing.openstreetmap.de): the coordinates of the start, end and pins, only when a stretch has to leave the NParks paths.
  • Toilets, water, shelters and sights — Overpass API (overpass-api.de and mirrors): the area around your route.
  • Weather — data.gov.sg (NEA): a general Singapore forecast request. Your location is not sent; the nearest area is picked in your browser.
  • “Open in Google Maps” and Google My Maps only happen if you click them; your route points then go to Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy and logs. “Use my location” is read by your browser and used only on the page — but once it becomes a start point, it is sent to the services above like any other point. If this page is hosted by a service such as GitHub, that host may also keep ordinary visit logs.

What is stored, and where

  • Saved walks and journal notes are kept only in your own browser (local storage) on this device, under this web address. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data deletes them — use Saved → Export backup to keep a copy.
  • PDFs, KML and GPX files are created on your device and go nowhere unless you share them.

Please use your judgement

Routes, distances, times, rest stops and weather notes are suggestions and estimates, not guarantees. Paths can be closed, flooded, unlit or unsuitable for your fitness or mobility; stairs and short road links are not always marked; places listed may be shut. Check NParks advisories before you go, watch for rain and lightning, carry water, and follow signs and the rules of each park. You walk at your own risk.
